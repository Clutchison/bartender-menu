## Matching rules and substitutions

- Match types rather than exact brands: Wild Turkey for bourbon, Bulleit for rye, Jameson for Irish whiskey, Meukow for cognac, E&J for brandy, Botanist for regular dry gin, Ketel One for vodka, and Lazzaroni for maraschino. Age/proof and flavor can differ from the named brands.
- Cointreau covers triple sec and ordinary orange curacao roles. In the Millionaire Royal it also replaces Grand Marnier, changing the brandy character. Drillaud white cacao covers creme de cacao; Mr Black covers coffee liqueur; Drillaud peach covers peach liqueur; Dempsey's covers Irish cream.
- Koloa covers aged/gold rum, including named Jamaican-aged and Demerara pours. This preserves the broad aged-rum type but changes flavor. Shrunken Skull uses it for both aged-rum portions, losing the original blend. Jonah's Curse covers spiced rum. Bacardi 151 covers the 151-proof role in Spanish Coffee.
- A **reposado-tequila recipe can be made with your anejo**, with more oak character. These are explicitly counted as practical aging-style substitutions, not exact reposado matches. Your blanco covers blanco recipes. If you want to preserve reposado specifically, those rows need reposado instead.
- White rum, unspiced dark/black rum, Jamaican overproof rum, Old Tom gin, navy-strength gin, and rhum agricole remain separate types. Malibu and Jonah's Curse do not stand in for white or unspiced rum. Bacardi 151 is not treated as a direct replacement for 57% or 63% Jamaican rum.
- Sweet red, dry, and blanc vermouth remain distinct. Lillet Blanc does not automatically cover Cocchi Americano or vermouth. Yellow Chartreuse is not silently replaced with green.
- Alcohol-containing bitters count as an additional alcoholic ingredient. The unidentified small Angostura bottle supplies no extra confirmed flavor. Fee Brothers confirms alcohol is present in its bitters' flavoring ingredients: [manufacturer FAQ](https://www.feebrothers.com/discover/).
- The linked Jasper's Mix, Chartreuse whipped cream, and Tom & Jerry batter were checked for alcoholic components. All their required alcohol can come from your stock under the substitutions above.
