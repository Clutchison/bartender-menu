# TCL NXTPAPER 14 — portrait wood frame

Design brief, September 27, 2026. User choices: NXTPAPER 14, wood frame, adapt a ready-made frame if practical, portrait. **Latest power decision: a 6–10-foot (2–3-metre) cable route to an outlet.** Keep the frame removable for service. Dimensions below distinguish published measurements, calculations, and proposed construction allowances. This is a fit-and-build plan; the aperture and holder are not ready for fabrication until the actual parts are measured.

## Recommended construction

Use an **11 × 14-inch wood frame with a deep rear recess**, a thin matte-black surround, and a removable tablet holder fastened to the frame. Hang the complete assembly on a lift-off cleat and supply power through a hidden rear cable exit. Remove the glazing. Bring the screen close to the front opening rather than leaving it at the back of a deep box.

For the visual direction, choose a low-sheen walnut or warm oak finish and black-core charcoal matboard. The dark surround hides most of the tablet bezel and blends into the menu background. A small amount of visible bezel is preferable to covering active pixels or blocking touch at the screen edge.

The decorative surround must not carry the tablet's weight. A screwed-in wooden or printed cradle carries the device; the frame carries the cradle; a cleat carries the complete assembly on the wall.

Source for body dimensions, weight, diagonal, and ratio: [TCL USA product specifications](https://us.tcl.com/products/nxtpaper-14). The diagonal is rounded and the screen has corner geometry, so measure the illuminated screen before ordering the surround. Include enough aperture clearance that a small alignment error does not cover pixels.

**“11 × 14” is the picture/content size, not the outside size.** A frame sold for an 8 × 10 image behind a mat can still have an 11 × 14 cavity; the included 8 × 10 mat is too small for this tablet's screen. Remove it. A frame whose outside dimensions are 11 × 14 is also too small for this design.

Rotate the tablet so its USB-C port points downward, then confirm that the menu can lock to that portrait orientation. A compact right-angle cable may fit within the 16.6 mm bottom clearance, but that is not confirmed. Measure the actual connected plug and its natural bend. If necessary, create a shallow channel in the rear edge of the frame for the cable, shift the tablet and surround opening slightly upward, or use a 12 × 16 frame. Do not force the plug or bend the cable sharply to preserve the nominal dimensions. Prefer a single suitable cable over a chain of adapters.

## Front-to-back arrangement

1. **Wood frame:** the visible finished surround and structural attachment point.
2. **Thin black surround:** black-core matboard for the first version, about 1–2 mm thick, with a custom aperture. Use a black cut edge. Keep it independently fixed to the frame and clear of active glass. It is cosmetic.
3. **Tablet:** as close to the aperture as practical, with screen-edge gestures accessible. No glass, acrylic cover, or touch overlay in front.
4. **Padded cradle:** corner pockets or brackets that retain the tablet in all directions, including when the frame is carried or laid down. Front stops and rear retainers are structural and independent of the cosmetic surround. Use small pads at perimeter contact points. Locate all contacts after inspecting the ports and buttons.
5. **Rear space and wall attachment:** provide connector clearance, a short cable service loop, strain relief, and a hidden exit. Mount the cleat to sound wood or a reinforced crosspiece attached to the frame sides. Leave the rear center open for air and camera clearance.

A practical DIY cradle can use short plywood brackets/blocks secured with screws into the wood frame or into two structural crosspieces. Bottom supports carry weight; side locators stop sliding; front stops and removable rear tabs capture the device gently without squeezing it. Any small front lips must sit outside the active display and under the decorative surround; the surround must never be the only front restraint. Leave the camera bump clear. The holders must not press on active glass, buttons, or a broad area of the tablet's back. A framer or maker can fabricate these parts after a dry fit; a 3D-printed insert is optional, not required.

Arrange the bottom supports on either side of the charging connector and clear of speaker openings. Secure the cable to the frame so pulls are not transmitted to the tablet port; leave a loose loop between the clip and the connector. Keep a comfortable grip on the wood frame for lifting it down.

Avoid a continuous foam envelope, a tightly clamped backboard, or adhesive directly on the tablet. Keep most of the rear open, with a path for air in at the bottom and out at the top. Test a normal menu session while charging before closing the enclosure or selecting a permanent screen-brightness setting.

## Microphones, buttons, and sensors

The manufacturer diagram places the power/volume/NXTPAPER controls and microphones along a long edge and speakers along the short edges. In portrait, the microphone/control edge becomes a vertical side. Use this to reserve an open channel around that side rather than wrapping the device tightly in foam. Exact hole and holder positions must come from the physical tablet. [TCL user manual, “Keys and connectors”](https://support-downloads.tcl.com/file/TCTDocument/User%20manual/01BTMKY5ZCWFNQSPFPIRH2TVIQUJDRWBQ5/TCL%20NXTPAPER%2014_9491G%20English%20UM-V0.2.pdf).

Provide fingertip access from the back or a small hidden notch to reach the power button. The frame should lift off its wall mount for service without disassembling the tablet holder. The black surround may cover a light/proximity sensor: locate it before cutting, leave it exposed if automatic brightness is wanted, or test a fixed-brightness setting. Do not assume covering the front camera also leaves every sensor unaffected.

## Wall mounting and power

Use a short two-piece metal French cleat, plus bottom spacers/bumpers to keep the frame steady under tapping. Attach the frame-side part to structural wood, not thin matboard or the supplied cardboard backing. Select fixings for the actual wall and the measured complete assembly weight; the tablet alone is 760 g. Test ordinary swipes for accidental lifting; if necessary, add a releasable catch. Keep the complete assembly removable for maintenance.

Leave sufficient clearance above the frame to lift it off the cleat. Match lower spacer depth to the top bracket, and preserve the ventilation openings. A single picture-wire or sawtooth hanger tends to allow more movement during touch use.

**Selected power route:** approximately 6–10 feet (2–3 metres) from outlet to frame, following corners/baseboards where practical. Use a paintable surface raceway for exposed wall sections. Keep the charger at the outlet, outside the tablet enclosure. Measure the route with string before choosing the final length. A 3-metre cable is a candidate only when the route plus service slack fits within 3 metres; a full ten-foot route needs extra length.

Choose a cable and adapter suitable for charging the exact tablet model over that distance. Prefer one appropriately rated USB-C cable; verify the manufacturer's charging specification at that length and test the actual combination. As evidence that this length is practical, StarTech lists a [3-metre USB-C cable rated for 60 W / 3 A PD](https://www.startech.com/en-eu/cables/usb2cc3mncbr); it is a straight-connector example, not a confirmed fit for the frame. A “USB-C” label or a data-extension function alone does not establish charging suitability. If the chosen run cannot sustain use, reconsider where power is supplied rather than adding arbitrary extension adapters.

A compact right-angle connector may reduce the required bottom clearance. [Example of the connector category, StarTech R2CCR-1M-USB-CABLE](https://www.startech.com/en-us/cables/r2ccr-1m-usb-cable); this one-metre example is not a selected long-run cable. Its power rating does not guarantee TCL's fastest charging mode. Use a compatible charger, confirm what comes with the regional SKU, and test that battery charge remains stable during a representative screen-on session. Enable a charge-limit or battery-protection feature if the installed firmware provides one; its presence has not been verified.

Keep the physical power key reachable and configure the screen to sleep or dim between bar sessions if appropriate. Leave enough slack to lift the frame slightly and disconnect power before taking it away. This plan uses a surface route; ordinary charging/extension cables are not being specified for concealment inside a wall.

## Assembly sequence

1. Measure the physical tablet, camera protrusion, illuminated display, buttons, microphones, and charging plug in the intended portrait position.
2. Make a paper/cardboard layout of an 11 × 14 cavity and approximately 8 × 12 screen opening. Check the menu's size on the wall and comfortable touch height.
3. Confirm the selected frame's true internal dimensions and disassembly before ordering or modifying it. Order frame-only if possible.
4. Dry-fit the tablet and selected connected cable with temporary supports on a padded bench. Verify connector clearance and access for unplugging, then mark the surround aperture from the final tablet position.
5. Fit the independent cradle and thin surround. Pad body contact points, relieve the camera, and keep ports and controls open. Finish/stain wood before installing electronics and let it cure.
6. Attach the cleat to structural parts, add bottom bumpers and cable strain relief, install the cable cover, and hang the complete frame. Check that there is room above it for lifting off and slack to disconnect power.
7. Test edge touches, wake/reboot access, speech capture, charging, and an extended normal menu session. Gently tilt the assembly over a padded surface to check that the cradle captures the tablet in every direction. Verify removal and rehanging without opening the frame or pulling the USB port.

## Brief for a framer or local maker

> I want to adapt a wood frame for a TCL NXTPAPER 14 in portrait. The body is nominally 222.02 × 322.41 × 6.95 mm and weighs 760 g. Start with an 11 × 14-inch clear content cavity and a deep rear recess. Use a matte wood finish and a black-core surround, with no front glass or acrylic. The surround opening will be measured from the actual lit screen, roughly 8 × 12 inches. Keep the screen near the front. Support the tablet in a padded cradle attached to the frame, independently of the cosmetic surround, with front and rear restraints. Leave the USB-C connector, microphones, buttons, speakers, camera bump, and ventilation clear. Mount the frame with a lift-off cleat for maintenance. Power will run from an outlet through a discreet rear cable exit: allow connector clearance, a service loop, and strain relief. Leave the power button accessible while mounted. Please verify all fits using the tablet and plugged-in cable before cutting the final surround.
